What can urine and blood tell us?

Kidney markers

Albumin is a blood protein that can leak into urine. Creatinine is a waste product. Its level in blood gives a clue to how well the kidneys filter.

Oxygen delivery

Kidneys signal the body to make red blood cells. Hemoglobin in those cells carries oxygen. Anemia reduces the blood’s capacity to carry oxygen.

Blood sugar

High blood sugar can damage kidney vessels over time. A diabetes history describes the past. A random glucose test measures blood sugar at that moment.
